# Identifier Catalog

## ECU-17

ECU-17 uses checksum A111 and pressure guard 80 kPa. This is the base controller, not the A variant.

## ECU-17A

ECU-17A uses checksum 9F33 and pressure guard 132 kPa. It belongs to safety envelope SIL-2.

## ECU-171

ECU-171 uses checksum B712 and pressure guard 91 kPa. It is retained for compatibility screening.

## MOD_88X

MOD_88X uses voltage threshold 42 mV and diagnostic owner Lumen Controls.

## MOD-88

MOD-88 uses voltage threshold 35 mV and diagnostic owner Legacy Controls.

## MOD-88X

MOD-88X uses voltage threshold 44 mV and diagnostic owner Delta Controls.

## VIN7K-02

VIN7K-02 uses calibration bucket BRAVO and checksum C0DE-02.

## VIN7K-020

VIN7K-020 uses calibration bucket CHARLIE and checksum C0DE-20.
