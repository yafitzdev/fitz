# Customer Support SLA

Gold support customers receive a first response within 15 minutes for Severity 1 incidents.
Silver support customers receive a first response within 2 business hours for Severity 1 incidents.
Bronze support customers receive a first response within 1 business day.

RMA means Return Material Authorization. Gold-tier RMA shipping labels expire after 14 days.
