# Data Retention Policy

Production ledger service tokens rotate every 30 days.

Analytics caches may retain pseudonymous event records for 90 days.

Biometric verification artifacts must be deleted within 24 hours.

EU customer export logs are retained for 180 days.

Any unencrypted production asset requires a security exception.
