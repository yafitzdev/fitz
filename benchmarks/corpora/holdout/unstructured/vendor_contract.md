# Vendor Contract Notes

Skyline Labs contracts auto-renew unless cancellation notice is sent at least
60 days before renewal. Skyline support credits are capped at 4 percent.

Cobalt CDN contracts include a security addendum requiring an annual SOC 2
report and a 30-day breach notice.

Blue Peak late invoices accrue a 1 percent late fee after the due date.
