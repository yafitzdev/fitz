# Observability SLA

Platinum customers reporting Severity 0 incidents receive acknowledgement within
5 minutes and a mitigation target of 30 minutes.

Gold customers reporting Severity 2 incidents receive first response within
2 hours.

GAR means Global Access Review. GAR reports expire after 21 days and must be
regenerated before an access audit can close.

P0 and P1 alerts route to pager. P2 alerts route to Slack. P3 alerts route to a
ticket queue.
