# Product Launch Status

## Project Atlas APAC

On 2026-03-04, Project Atlas entered a pilot in Singapore and Seoul. The pilot
was not customer general availability.

On 2026-07-18, Project Atlas reached limited general availability in APAC with
the semantic ranker enabled.

## Project Zephyr EU

On 2026-06-11, Project Zephyr remained in EU beta because the privacy banner
dependency had not cleared. The final GA date was not announced.

## Deprecations

The final retirement date for `legacy_sync` is 2026-09-30.
