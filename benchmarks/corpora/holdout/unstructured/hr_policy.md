# People Operations Policy

## Time Off

Independent contractors do not accrue company PTO. Full-time employees receive
20 working days of PTO each calendar year.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers
receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave.

## Travel Expenses

The travel meal cap is 65 USD per day. Receipts are required for any individual
expense above 25 USD.
