# Access Policy

## Account Rotation

Service accounts rotate API tokens every 45 days. Human employee tokens rotate
every 90 days.

Break-glass access grants expire after 4 hours and require an incident ticket.

## Guest Access

Guest collaborators can view Amber project boards but cannot export customer
data. Archived accounts cannot receive new export grants.

## MFA Exceptions

2026-03-10: Pilot users under exception MFA-CX-13 may skip hardware keys until
renewal.

2026-08-02: Current rule: no active MFA exceptions remain for customer data
systems.
