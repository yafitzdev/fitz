# Procurement Policy

Vendors with annual spend above 50000 USD require Security review before
renewal.

MeridianAI contract auto-renewal cancellation requires 75 days written notice.

Evaluation licenses expire after 30 days unless Procurement converts them to a
paid agreement.

Purchase orders under 2500 USD can be approved by Team Leads. Purchase orders
of 2500 USD or above need Finance Ops approval.
